Maintainer resilience

Can the project survive losing its top maintainer?
28 Critical

The classic bus-factor risk: how many people the project actually depends on. A single dominant maintainer is the most common failure mode of open-source projects — one person burning out, changing jobs, or walking away can end the project.

How it's scored
60 ptsBus factor — contributors needed to cover 50% of commits; 1 scores very low, 5+ scores high
25 ptsDistribution — the smaller the top contributor's share of commits, the better
15 ptsContributor breadth — total contributors; 10+ saturates
Weight in overall score: 20%
