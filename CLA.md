# Contributor Licence Agreement

**This is a draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Read it, and if you
are contributing on behalf of an employer, have them read it too.**

## Why this exists

inspect-scanner is released under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Some users cannot accept
AGPL — its network clause reaches their hosted service — and they buy a
commercial licence instead. That revenue is what pays for the project.

Selling such a licence requires the right to license *every line* in the
release. Contributions do not grant that by default: you keep the copyright in
your patch, and AGPL alone would not let anyone, including us, ship it under
other terms. This agreement supplies the missing permission — and nothing else.

**It is a licence grant, not an assignment.** You keep the copyright in your
work. You may use, publish and relicense your own contribution however you like,
to anyone, for ever. Signing takes nothing away from you.

## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

Once per person, not per pull request. If you are signing for a company rather
than for yourself, say so in the same comment and name the company, and we will
follow up.

If any part of this stops you from contributing, open an issue and say which
part. A contribution lost to an unread paragraph helps nobody.
